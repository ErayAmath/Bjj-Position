      Open problem

      The frame counter restarts 15 times, giving 16 segments — camera views of 6 sparring sequences, recovered from their near-identical position mix. Splitting by segment would leak the same moment seen from another angle. But splitting by sequence has its own catch: each sequence drilled only a few positions (back exists only in sequence 6), so whole classes would vanish from training. Choosing the split is the next decision.

      05 — Roadmap

      Four stripes to go

      1. M1

        Keypoint baseline

        Single-frame classifier on keypoints, honest split by recording, baselines, an MLP in PyTorch, confusion matrix.

      2. M2

        Time

        Positions last seconds, not frames. Use neighbouring frames: smoothing, then a temporal model.

      3. M3

        My own footage

        Pose estimation on my own sparring videos — and working out which skeleton is me.

      4. M4

        Training stats

        Time per position, transitions, where I get passed. The reason this project exists.
